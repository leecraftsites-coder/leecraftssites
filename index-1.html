Service terms

Basic information for prospective clients.

Quotes and project scope

Package prices are starting prices for the services described. The final scope, deliverables, price and timeline must be agreed in writing before a project begins. Work outside the agreed scope may require an additional quote.

Payment

Payment is by EFT / bank transfer. The payment amount, due dates and any deposit arrangement will be confirmed with the client in writing before work starts. Do not send payment until you have received and accepted the project quote.

Revisions and timing

Each listed package includes two revisions. A revision means a reasonable adjustment to the agreed design; new features or a change in project scope may be quoted separately. Delivery time depends on project size and how quickly the required content and feedback are supplied.

Third-party costs and launch

Domain registration, paid hosting, premium assets, subscriptions and other third-party services are not included unless specifically stated in the written quote. The client and LeeCraftsSites should agree who owns and manages any accounts required for launch.

Acceptance

A project only proceeds once the client has reviewed and accepted the written quote and project arrangements. These general website notes do not replace a project-specific agreement.
